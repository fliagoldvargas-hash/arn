# Neurix Page Topology

Target: https://www.neurix.sh

## Global Layout
- Fixed 60px top navigation, black translucent background at load and stronger blur after scroll > 80px.
- Single black page with subtle vertical grid lines at 25%, 50%, and 75%.
- Serif editorial headings, uppercase micro labels, thin white borders, low-contrast white body text.
- Page height extracted at desktop: 4332px.

## Sections
1. Navigation: fixed overlay with Neurix mark, Build/Marketplace/Docs links, theme icon button, Connect button.
2. Hero: full-screen-plus introduction, cyan/purple abstract ribbons, two-line serif headline, CTA buttons, market ticker, powered-by row.
3. Platform: centered intro followed by 5-card bordered grid. First two cards span half width, final three span thirds.
4. Marketplace: centered editorial heading and descriptive body copy with a single text link.
5. Agent Labs: centered intro and three metric cards.
6. Ecosystem: centered intro, four protocol metric cards, copyable contract pill.
7. CTA: large final callout with two buttons.
8. Footer: compact uppercase links, ecosystem names, copyright line.

## Interaction Models
- Navigation: scroll-driven visual state.
- Buttons and links: hover state through border/color changes.
- Contract pill: click-driven copy feedback.
- No videos or canvas were detected.
