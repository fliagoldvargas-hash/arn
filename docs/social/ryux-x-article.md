# What is RYUX? The Platform for Tokenized Autonomous Agents

Header image: `docs/social/assets/ryux-minimal-02-tokenized.png`

## Article

AI agents are becoming workers.

Tokens are becoming ownership layers.

RYUX exists to connect both into a new on-chain economy.

The idea is simple: if autonomous agents can do useful digital work, generate activity, hold assets, improve over time, and serve communities, then they need more than a prompt box. They need infrastructure. They need ownership. They need markets.

RYUX is building the platform for tokenized autonomous agents on Solana.

## The problem

AI agents are moving fast.

They can write, trade, analyze, automate, communicate, monitor data, and run workflows across the internet. Every month, they become more capable. But the economy around them is still early.

Right now, there is no clean place where a builder can launch an agent as a real on-chain project, connect it to useful skills, give it a treasury, and make it discoverable to the market.

There is also no clean place where people can discover autonomous agents, inspect what they are doing, track their metrics, and decide which projects deserve attention.

That is the gap RYUX is being built to fill.

## The RYUX thesis

The next wave of crypto will not only be about tokens.

It will be about what those tokens are connected to.

A token can be more than a ticker. It can represent ownership, access, coordination, incentives, and community around an autonomous system.

An AI agent can be more than a tool. It can become a digital business that performs useful work, earns revenue, grows its treasury, and evolves over time.

RYUX brings those two ideas together.

Tokenized autonomous agents.
Built on Solana.

## How it works

RYUX connects five core layers:

1. **Agents**

Autonomous systems that can perform digital work: trading, research, community management, analytics, content, automation, and more.

2. **Tokens**

Ownership and coordination layers for agent projects. A token can give a community a direct way to follow, support, and participate in an agent's growth.

3. **Skills**

Modular capabilities that agents can use to become more useful. Think trading tools, social tools, data tools, revenue tools, automation tools, and custom workflows.

4. **Treasuries**

On-chain wallets that make agent activity more transparent. Instead of black-box promises, projects can point to visible assets, flows, and performance.

5. **Marketplace**

A discovery layer where autonomous agents can be listed, tracked, compared, and explored.

Together, these layers turn an agent from a private script into an on-chain project.

## Why Solana

Autonomous agents need fast, low-cost infrastructure.

If agents are going to interact with markets, treasuries, skills, and communities in real time, the chain underneath them needs to support frequent activity without making every action expensive or slow.

That is why RYUX is being built on Solana.
