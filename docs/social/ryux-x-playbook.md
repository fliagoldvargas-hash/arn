# RYUX X Playbook

## Brand profile

RYUX should feel like a serious crypto-native infrastructure brand, not a meme page.

Core identity:
- Category: on-chain AI agent infrastructure on Solana.
- Promise: build, discover, and invest in autonomous agents that can do real digital work.
- Main line: Software that thinks. Agents that earn.
- Short explanation: RYUX connects tokens, agents, skills, treasuries, and marketplace discovery into one agent economy layer.
- Current stage: pre-launch / introduction. Use "coming soon", "building", "begins here", and "more soon" until the actual launch is confirmed.

Voice:
- Clean, direct, technical, slightly cinematic.
- Short sentences. No hype spam. No fake certainty.
- Use "build", "agents", "on-chain", "Solana", "skills", "treasury", "marketplace", "verified", "autonomous businesses".
- Avoid: guaranteed returns, "100x", "moon", financial promises, over-explaining the same thing in every post.

Visual identity:
- Background: black / near black.
- Primary mark: three glowing white rounded triangles.
- Accent system: mostly black and white. Keep cyan `#18d0d6`, purple `#bf5fff`, orange `#fb8b42`, and green `#4ade80` for the website, but use them rarely on X visuals.
- Layout: pure black background, glowing white mark, large serif type, very short phrases, lots of negative space.

## Content pillars

1. Thesis: explain why autonomous agents need an economy layer.
2. Product: show how RYUX works: token, agent, skills, treasury, marketplace.
3. Builder education: agent use cases, skill modules, dashboard, launch flow.
4. Investor education: discovery, verified agents, metrics, on-chain treasury, DYOR.
5. Build-in-public: roadmap, shipped milestones, launch notes, screenshots.
6. Ecosystem: Solana, Pump.fun, Meteora, Jupiter, agent tooling, partnerships.

## Posting rhythm

For launch phase:
- 2-4 posts per day.
- 1 thread every 2-3 days.
- 1 roadmap/product visual every day until marketplace opens.
- Reply daily to Solana, AI agent, Pump.fun, and builder conversations.

Daily mix:
- Morning: thesis or educational post.
- Afternoon: product/roadmap post with visual.
- Night: short community post, quote, or question.

## Thread idea: What is RYUX?

```text
1/ RYUX is infrastructure for tokenized autonomous agents on Solana.

The simple version:
we are turning AI agents into on-chain businesses that can be built, funded, discovered, and tracked.

2/ The problem:

AI agents can do more digital work every month.

But there is no clean place to launch one as an investable business, connect it to real capabilities, and let the market verify what it does.

3/ RYUX connects five pieces:

- agent infrastructure
- token ownership
- skill modules
- on-chain treasuries
- marketplace discovery

4/ For builders:

Launch agents, add skills, connect workflows, deploy on cloud infrastructure, and build systems that can generate activity and revenue.

5/ For investors:

Discover agents, inspect metrics, follow treasuries, track performance, and avoid blind speculation.

6/ The first phase is simple:

RYUX is being introduced.
The marketplace comes next.
Dashboard follows after that.
Builder layer starts opening later.

7/ The long-term vision:

Software that does not just follow orders.

Software that runs itself, earns money, improves over time, and becomes a new kind of on-chain asset.

That is RYUX.
```
